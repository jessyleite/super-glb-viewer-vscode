2. Data We Collect

2.1 Analytics Data (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics (Google Ireland Limited) to understand how visitors use the Service. Google Analytics collects:

  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring website
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and screen resolution
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level)
  • Anonymized IP address

This data is processed by Google under their Privacy Policy. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for improving the Service.

2.2 Email Address (Newsletter — Optional)

If you subscribe to receive updates about Super GLB Exporter, we collect your email address via Mailchimp (Intuit Inc.). This data is processed by Mailchimp under their Privacy Policy. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in each email.

2.3 3D Model Files (Sharing Feature — Optional)

When you use the "Share" feature, the GLB model file you choose to share is uploaded to our server (super-glb-uploader.jessyleite.dev) and stored on our CDN (assets.jessyleite.dev). The shared model becomes accessible via a public URL. We do not associate uploaded files with any personal identity.

Important: Only use the Share feature for files you own or have the right to distribute. Shared models may be publicly accessible to anyone with the link.

2.4 Local Storage (IndexedDB)

The Service stores the following data locally in your browser using IndexedDB. This data never leaves your device:

  • Selected rendering engine and backend preferences
  • Advanced display options (background color, exposure, tonemapping)
  • Custom HDR environment files you upload

You can clear this data at any time using the "Reset to Defaults" button in the application settings, or by clearing your browser data.

2.5 Data We Do NOT Collect

The core functionality of Super GLB Viewer operates entirely in your browser. Specifically:

  • 3D models you load for viewing are not uploaded to any server (unless you explicitly use the Share feature)
  • Model inspection, editing, optimization, comparison, and export are performed entirely client-side
  • We do not require account creation, login, or any form of registration
  • We do not collect names, addresses, payment information, or any personal identifiers

4. International Data Transfers

Some of our third-party service providers (Google, Mailchimp/Intuit) are based in the United States. Data transfers to the US are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. By using the Service, you acknowledge that your analytics data may be processed outside the European Economic Area.

5. Data Retention

  • Analytics data: Retained by Google Analytics for 14 months, then automatically deleted.
  • Email addresses: Retained until you unsubscribe from the newsletter.
  • Shared models: Stored on our CDN indefinitely. Contact us to request deletion of a shared model.
  • Local storage: Persists in your browser until you clear it.
