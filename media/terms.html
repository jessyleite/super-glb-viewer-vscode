1. Description of the Service

Super GLB Viewer is a free, browser-based 3D model viewer that allows you to:

  • View and inspect GLB and glTF 3D model files
  • Switch between rendering engines (Three.js, Babylon.js, PlayCanvas) and backends (WebGL2, WebGPU)
  • Edit materials, textures, and transforms of 3D models
  • Compare models side by side
  • Optimize and compress 3D models
  • Export modified models
  • Play and control animations and blend shapes
  • Customize rendering settings (HDR environments, exposure, tonemapping)
  • Share models via a public URL
  • Load models from external URLs via query parameters

All viewing, inspection, editing, optimization, comparison, and export operations are performed entirely in your browser. No model data is sent to our servers unless you explicitly use the Share feature.
